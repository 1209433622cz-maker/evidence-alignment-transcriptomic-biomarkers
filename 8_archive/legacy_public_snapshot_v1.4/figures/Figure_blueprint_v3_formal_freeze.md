# Nature-style figure blueprint v3 — formal A1R2 freeze

**Date:** 13 September 2026  
**Principle:** visual hierarchy follows the outer-primary evidence audit; the frozen randomized benchmark is nested rather than presented as the entire claim universe.

## Global production standard

- White background, restrained blue/orange/vermillion/grey palette, colour-blind-safe encodings.
- Minimum final text size 7 pt; panel labels 9–10 pt bold; titles 9–11 pt.
- Main-figure target width 178 mm; single-column alternatives 89 mm only where labels remain legible.
- Vector PDF and SVG are authoritative; PNG is visual-QA output at publication-grade resolution.
- Direct labels are preferred. Legends must not cover data or headers.
- Zero/no-effect references are neutral grey; significance is never encoded by colour alone.
- Every data-bearing panel must be generated from a versioned source table. No hand-entered scientific count is permitted.
- Figure 1–3 stay explicitly `ARCHITECTURE_ONLY` until registered A2 extraction is complete.

## Figure 1 | Systematic universe and nested-benchmark bridge

- **a, PRISMA-ScR flow:** records, deduplicated records, reports, eligible reports and claim-estimands. Counts remain pending A2.
- **b, independent evidence axes:** formula executability, randomized opportunity, estimand alignment and lawful data path are displayed as intersecting/parallel axes, not a false sequential funnel.
- **c, entity graph:** report → claim-estimand, biomarker definition, trial/dataset and implementation linkage.
- **d, nested bridge:** outer eligible universe → R5A-evaluable subset → 15 local claims / 27 frozen models, with the pre-existing nested counts clearly labelled as purposive rather than field prevalence.

## Figure 2 | Claim language and original supporting evidence

- Matrix of claim-language class by original evidence design.
- Side field reports whether a formal treatment-by-biomarker interaction was available: yes, no or indeterminate.
- Cells remain pending until dual extraction/adjudication.
- A claim such as “sensitive” is not visually equated to randomized predictive evidence.

## Figure 3 | Multi-axis readiness without a composite score

- Rows are `claim_estimand_id`; columns are formula, fidelity, randomized opportunity, estimand compatibility, lawful data path, independent evaluation and interval support.
- Fidelity uses F0–F4 with symbol and text redundancy.
- Columns retain independent missing/indeterminate states.
- No traffic-light total, weighted sum or rank order is allowed.

## Figure 4 | Claim-centred nested randomized benchmark

- Fifteen claim groups are the visual unit; trial-level models are nested beneath each claim.
- Binary OR and survival HR estimates are separated by context; full intervals are shown.
- Existing 27-row model forest moves to Supplementary/Extended Data.
- Nominal results are marked without implying family-adjusted replication.

**Current A1R2 status:** data-bearing source and PDF/SVG/PNG produced from `Figure4_nested_claim_forest_source.tsv`.

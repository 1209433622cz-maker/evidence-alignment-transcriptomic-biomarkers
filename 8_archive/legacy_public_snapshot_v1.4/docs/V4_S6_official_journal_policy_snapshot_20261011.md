## BMC Medical Research Methodology

### Official facts

- The journal publishes original research on methodological approaches to healthcare research, including study design, data quality/reporting, statistics and modelling, and explicitly welcomes empirical studies connecting methodological choices with study outcomes.
- Its Research Article criteria accept original primary research or new computational methods/tests/procedures, and systematic reviews that follow reporting guidance.
- The same page states that non-commissioned pooled analyses of selected published research and bibliometric analyses are not considered.
- The structured abstract must be no more than 350 words and use Background, Methods, Results and Conclusions.
- Three to ten keywords, a separate Conclusions section, a list of abbreviations and the full Declarations block are required.
- LLMs cannot be authors and their use must be documented in Methods.
- General formatting asks for double spacing, page and line numbering, editable DOCX/RTF/TeX, machine-readable data where applicable and a data-availability statement.
- Current APC shown on the journal page: GBP 2,290 / USD 3,090 / EUR 2,690, determined at acceptance and subject to taxes and country-tier rules.
- Figure titles are limited to 15 words, legends to 300 words, individual files to 10 MB; 170 mm is the full-page width and approximately 300 dpi is requested at final size.

Official pages:

- https://link.springer.com/journal/12874/aims-and-scope
- https://link.springer.com/journal/12874/submission-guidelines
- https://link.springer.com/journal/12874/submission-guidelines/research-article

### Project-fit judgement

The methodological purpose, explicit provenance audit and A0-A4 specification reconstruction fit the journal's scope well. The principal editorial risk is classification: the bounded study reuses selected published/public resources, although it is not a pooled meta-analysis. Therefore this is a **conditional first choice**, not a frozen target, and an article-type enquiry remains advisable before formal submission.

## Journal of Clinical Epidemiology

### Official facts

- The publisher describes the journal as advancing health-care decision making through metascience and innovative methods.
- It publishes methods-focused work across primary research, evidence synthesis, guidelines and implementation science, including registered reports, protocols and simulation studies.

Official publisher pages:

- https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-clinical-epidemiology/0895-4356
- https://www.us.elsevierhealth.com/journal-of-clinical-epidemiology-0895-4356.html

### Project-fit judgement

The claim-estimand framework, versioned correction pathways and inferential-eligibility focus are conceptually aligned. The likely editorial bar is higher and the purposive oncology-only object set may be judged too narrow unless the methods contribution and transferable recommendations are made exceptionally explicit. This is a **methods-journal stretch option**. Article type and current detailed formatting must be rechecked if selected.

## npj Precision Oncology

### Official facts

- Scope includes precision-oncology biomarkers, pharmacogenomics and secondary analyses of clinical-trial datasets.
- The scope specifically highlights biomarker studies with biological and external validation in an independent cohort.
- Initial npj submissions do not need final formatting, but editorial screening considers novelty, significance and technical quality.
- Original Research APC shown on the journal page: GBP 3,190 / USD 4,290 / EUR 3,490, determined at acceptance and subject to taxes and waiver/funding rules.
- Nature Portfolio requires transparent data availability for the minimum dataset and disclosure of restrictions.
- Current Nature Portfolio AI policy keeps accountability human, permits assistive/evaluative use with oversight, and expects transparent disclosure; AI cannot replace scholarly judgement.

Official pages:

- https://www.nature.com/npjprecisiononcology/aims
- https://www.nature.com/npjprecisiononcology/for-authors-and-referees/editorial-process
- https://www.nature.com/npjprecisiononcology/open-access
- https://www.nature.com/npjprecisiononcology/for-authors-and-referees/about/editorial-policies/reporting-standards
- https://www.nature.com/nature/editorial-policies/ai

### Project-fit judgement

The disease area and trial-secondary-analysis components fit, but the paper is an empirical methods/provenance study rather than a new biomarker with independent biological validation. This is an **oncology stretch option** with a material desk-reject risk.

## BMC Cancer

### Official facts

- Scope includes cancer molecular biology, genetics, epidemiology, clinical trials and biomarker studies.
- Its Research Article page states that manuscripts consisting solely of bioinformatics, computational analysis or predictions from public databases without validation will not be considered.
- The same page excludes non-commissioned pooled analyses of selected published research.

Official pages:

- https://link.springer.com/journal/12885/aims-and-scope
- https://link.springer.com/journal/12885/submission-guidelines/research-article

### Project-fit judgement

Although the oncology topic fits, the current paper could be interpreted as public-resource computation without the form of external validation expected by the journal. It is **not recommended as the immediate backup** unless the editorial office explicitly confirms that the empirical methodology design falls outside that exclusion.

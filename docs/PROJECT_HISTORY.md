# Development history

## Candidate directions stopped by prospective gates

- **TGCT cisplatin resistance:** bulk replication and single-cell localization
  gates failed; final NO-GO.
- **GIST imatinib resistance:** discovery-to-replication direction was negative
  and pathway concordance failed; final NO-GO without WGCNA/ML rescue.
- **GBM bevacizumab:** randomized resources were valuable, but a generic new
  biomarker paper was not novel or independently supported; NO-GO as a product.
- **HER2 dual blockade:** ten published signatures were reconstructed in
  TRIO-US B07 and CHER-LOB; direction agreement did not produce strict positive
  replication; NO-GO for a single biomarker-validation paper.

## Strategic transition

The repeated NO-GO outcomes revealed a general methodological problem: many
published predictive claims are based on treated-arm response or prognostic
association rather than a randomized estimate of differential treatment
benefit. The project therefore shifted from discovering a favourable biomarker
to auditing claim/evidence/estimand alignment and frozen re-executability.

## A1R2 historical form

The project next used an outer systematic evidence-audit architecture and a
nested frozen randomized benchmark. That stage produced the historical 27-model
table, the 23-model absolute-effect sensitivity and the first public aggregate
release. These files remain versioned evidence, not discarded work.

## RP-Q2 correction and integration

Source and population review then established that the public TRIO records do
not identify assignment phase at participant level. Historical calculations
were preserved while their randomized-inference role was downgraded. AVAglio
source alignment recovered the 349→339→333 population lineage and separated
unadjusted, adjusted and ridge specifications.

## Current RPv4 form

RPv4 defines a finite retrospective methods study centered on 22 object-level
changes in inferential eligibility, the TRIO identifiability boundary and a
single AVAglio incremental model stage. S0/S1 froze the inputs and role table
before A2 fitting.

V4-S2 executed the two prespecified AVAglio A2 Cox interactions on the fixed
333-participant population and reproduced them independently in R. V4-S3 then
closed the TRIO branch at design-level partial identifiability without guessing
participant phase. V4-S4/S5 integrated the 22 objects and rebuilt the current
manuscript and four-figure set. V4-S6/S6H1 completed journal and machine-level
submission preparation while preserving eleven real-author truth categories as
open.

Formal full-field search is no longer the immediate execution milestone. The
public aggregate repository is synchronized through S6H1, but submission
remains on hold until the missing author/accountability facts and final WPS-only
upload QA are complete.
